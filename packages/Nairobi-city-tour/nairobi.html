Nairobi City Tour

Tour Itinerary

You will meet the tour guide in the morning at the hotel’s reception. Next, you will visit the Giraffe Centre, then go to the Elephant Orphanage. You can have lunch packed boxes, at a restaurant, or stop at a lodge in the park for lunch. Without leaving the park, you can start your safari in the Nairobi National Park till evening. After completing the safari, you will be taken back to your hotel. On your way, you will experience the hustle and bustle of the capital city.

NB: The type of city tour program will largely depend on the location of your hotel in the city and the amount of time available for the tour.

Schedule

  • Safari: 06:30 - 10:30
  • Elephant Orphanage: 11:00 - 12:00
  • Lunch: 12:30 - 13:30
