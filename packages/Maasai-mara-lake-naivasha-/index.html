Maasai Mara, Lake Naivasha & Lake Nakuru

Day 1: Arrival – To Hotel in Nairobi

Arrival JKIA airport, picked, and transferred to hotel.

Dinner and overnight at the hotel.

Depending on the arrival you can opt to go giraffe center or do a half day city tour of Nairobi.

Day 2: Nairobi to Maasai Mara

After an early morning breakfast you will be picked from the hotel and depart for Maasai Mara via the Great Rift Valley view point.

Arrive at the Maasai Mara in time for lunch and unwind.

Later go for afternoon game drive in the Mara reserve. Return back to camp for dinner.

Day 3: Maasai Mara Full Day Game

After breakfast the game drive starts for the full day. Explore Maasai Mara in search of the big five and other wild animals.

You will have a picnic lunch in the park overlooking the vast landscape of the park.

Return to the hotel for dinner after the afternoon game drive.

Day 4: Maasai Mara to Lake Naivasha

Have breakfast, check out and depart for Lake Naivasha, a fresh water lake.

Arrive at lunch time. Enjoy lunch and unwind.

Go for the afternoon game drive with an option for boat ride for hippo spotting or walk safari at Crescent Island with giraffes and zebras.

Dinner and overnight at Naivasha.

Day 5: Lake Naivasha to Lake Nakuru

After breakfast, check out of the lodge and depart for Lake Nakuru which is well known for pink flamingos and rhinos in their natural habitat.

Lunch, dinner and overnight at a Lake Nakuru lodge.

Day 6: Lake Nakuru to Nairobi

After breakfast drive to Nairobi.

Afternoon check in to your hotel for dinner and overnight.

Day 7: Departure Day

You will be picked from your hotel to the airport for your departure.

End Of Safari.
