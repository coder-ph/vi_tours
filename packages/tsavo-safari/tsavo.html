TSAVO NATIONAL PARK

TSAVO ONE DAY SAFARI FROM MOMBASA

  • Early morning pick up from hotel.
  • On the way, enjoy a beautiful sunrise.
  • A 20 minute stop over for breakfast before proceeding.
  • Arrive at the Batchuma gate to enter the Tsavo East National park.
  • The game drive starts immediately upon arrival.
  • Then guests break for lunch at lodges inside the park or right next to it.
  • From lunch we go for afternoon game drive.
  • Exit the gate and depart back to your hotel in Mombasa/ Diani in time for dinner.

    • NB: This safari can also be done to Tsavo West especially for travelers seeking scenic landscapes hills. From Tsavo West, you be able to see the tallest Mountain in Afrika ,Kilimanjaro.
    Learn about Mzima spring. A spring that is the main source of water to Mombasa and it environs. Also a home for hippos.
